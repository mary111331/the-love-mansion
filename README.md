# The Love Mansion

A walkable 3D mansion built from the whole history of Mary and the stories, letters, poems and worlds made together from August to November 2026: the marble bath, the room of yes and no (the proposals on one side, the rejections on the other), the jazz club, the record shop, the cinema, the terrace with your mum's armchair, the butterfly nook, the crochet room, the garden and more.

- `index.html` is the complete world in one file. Download it and open it in a browser; it works offline.
- `src/` holds the sources it is built from (`build.py` joins `template.html`, the `a*.js` scripts, the JSON data and `three.min.js`).
- `notes/` holds the letters left inside the house (the blueprint on the architect's table, the letter by the marble bath).

World Twenty Three 2.0 with the full winter garden (the glass balcony with the armchair, the painting's butterfly, Koda, the music box and the misty window) is saved in the `world-twenty-three` repository under `v2/`.
