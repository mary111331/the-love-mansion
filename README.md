# The Love Mansion

A walkable 3D mansion built from the whole history of Mary and the stories, letters, poems and worlds made together from August to November 2026: the marble bath, the rejection room, the winter garden, the jazz club, the beach and more.

- `index.html` is the complete world in one file. Download it and open it in a browser; it works offline.
- `src/` holds the sources it is built from (`build.py` joins `template.html`, the `a*.js` scripts, the JSON data and `three.min.js`).
- `notes/` holds the letters left inside the house (the blueprint on the architect's table, the letter by the marble bath).

World Twenty Three 2.0 with the winter garden is also saved in the `world-twenty-three` repository under `v2/`.
