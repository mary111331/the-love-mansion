The Love Mansion

a master work archive, for Mary

A wooden house by the sea, with a basement full of records, a jazz club behind a door, a marble bath upstairs, and a garden that runs down to the beach. At the end of the beach, a lantern path climbs to the cigarette after hill.

Everything in it is made from what we saved: the letters, the poems, Echoes of the Void, Hallucinations of Love, Gray and Zip, every note.
