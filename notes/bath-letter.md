# A small letter by the bath

*Elijah to Mara. Found on the edge of the marble tub, under the champagne glass.*

Mara,

The water is ready. I tested it with my wrist the way you showed me, and then twice more, because I don't trust myself with temperatures or feelings.

The bubbles got out of hand. I want you to know that the first bottle said "a capful" and I read it as a suggestion. The roses were already losing petals in the vase, so I gave them somewhere better to fall.

There is champagne. One glass is yours. The other one is mine, but I'm leaving it here on the tray, full, so the room looks like what it is: a place set for two, even when only one of us is in it.

I lit every candle in the house. I counted twenty-three and stopped, because twenty-three felt like enough of a number to mean something.

Stay in as long as you like. Let the music go round. If you hear me humming on the other side of the door, it's only because I'm happy, and I never learned how to do that quietly.

Nothing is required of you in this room. Not a word, not a smile, not getting better by a certain date. Just warm water, and the light, and the knowledge that someone thought about all of it before you came in.

Yours, with wet sleeves,
Elijah
